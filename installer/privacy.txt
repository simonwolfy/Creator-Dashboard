Creator Intelligence stores analytics, media metadata, settings, and backups in the workspace you select during first-run setup.

Provider credentials are stored in the operating system credential vault. They are not bundled with the application or written to the workspace database.

Uninstalling Creator Intelligence removes the application but preserves your selected workspace. You may delete that workspace separately when you no longer need its data.

FFmpeg is optional and is not bundled. The app can detect an existing installation, guide you to a local binary folder, or offer a Winget installation from its FFmpeg settings page.

Full privacy notice: https://github.com/simonwolfy/Creator-Dashboard/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
