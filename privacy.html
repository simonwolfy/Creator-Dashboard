Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Simon's Creator Cove, powered by the Creator Intelligence desktop application ("the App"), handles information. The App is currently operated for the personal use of its owner and does not provide a hosted user-account service.

Information the App accesses

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the App may request the user.info.basic and video.list permissions. These permissions allow the App to read basic TikTok profile information and the list and metadata of videos authorized by the account owner. The App does not request permission to publish, edit, or delete TikTok videos.

How information is used

Authorized TikTok information is used only to display, organize, and analyze the owner's creator content and performance inside the App. It is not used for advertising, sold, rented, or shared with data brokers.

Storage and security

Creator data is stored locally in a workspace selected on the owner's Windows computer. Provider credentials, including access and refresh tokens, are stored using the operating system's credential vault rather than in the workspace database. The App currently has no product-usage telemetry, advertising tracker, or automatic crash-reporting service.

Sharing

The App sends requests to TikTok only as needed to authenticate the authorized account, refresh authorization, synchronize permitted information, or revoke access. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and security.

When the owner runs Setup Once, the App contacts Windows Package Manager sources to obtain FFmpeg and Hugging Face to obtain the selected open-source Whisper model. These downloads do not include creator content, workspace data, platform credentials, or account identifiers.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains until it is deleted by the owner or removed by a configured local retention rule. The owner may disconnect TikTok in the App, revoke access through TikTok account settings, and delete the local workspace data. Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete an external workspace.

Third-party services

Use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policies. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
