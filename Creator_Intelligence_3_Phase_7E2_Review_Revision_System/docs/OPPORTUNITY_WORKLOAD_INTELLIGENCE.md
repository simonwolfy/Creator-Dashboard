# VOD Opportunity and Editor Workload Intelligence

Phase 7.0D.3 answers two operational questions:

1. Which VOD should be edited next?
2. Does the editor have enough capacity to take it?

## Opportunity score

The score combines highlight quality, number of strong moments, expected Shorts,
long-form opportunities, content density, historical source performance, and
expected outputs per editor hour.

## Capacity forecast

The editor forecast estimates queued hours, weekly capacity, available hours,
utilization, likely clear date, overdue work, and risk level.

## Assignment recommendations

VODs are ranked by opportunity and capacity fit. The system recommends either
`Assign now` or `Hold or reduce scope`, with the expected output and workload.

These are planning estimates. They do not replace real editor time tracking.
